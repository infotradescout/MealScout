# OR / LISA doctrine pointer

This repo inherits Operational Reality and LISA doctrine from the shared
canon. Do not fork a second mission statement here.

## Mission (short)

We are **not** selling “AI.” We sell a better way to operate:

- Creators / users stay in control
- AI **assists** appropriate tasks — does **not** replace decision-making
- **Assist, don’t replace**
- Reduce storage, drift, and compute waste — get ~10× better results from
  what is current and real (OR / LISA / 4data), not blank-prompt churn

## Selective inheritance (the model)

Generic AI regenerates. OR **inherits selectively**: keep what is still
true and human-controlled; discard stale waste, harvest theater, and
decision replacement. Product keep/discard packets are instances of this
model.
