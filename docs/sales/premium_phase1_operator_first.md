# Profile-First Operator Plan

This document replaces the retired Premium subscription plan.

## Goal

Make each merchant profile accurate, operational, and valuable enough that owners keep it current and customers trust its actions.

## Product law

- The profile is the product.
- Every merchant receives the complete profile toolset.
- Access is a non-expiring free trial with no card and no monthly bill.
- No tool is unlocked by paying for a profile tier.
- Transaction charges remain separate and are disclosed in their own workflows.

## Operator activation

An activated profile has:

1. Confirmed identity and ownership
2. Real logo, cover, and representative media
3. Current menu or offerings
4. Current hours, dated schedule, location, or service area
5. A truthful primary customer action
6. Operational ordering or booking prerequisites when those actions are enabled

## Success measures

- Time to useful public profile
- Owner-confirmed profile rate
- Weekly schedule/menu/location update rate
- Profile-to-action conversion
- Successfully fulfilled orders and bookings
- Payment and payout reconciliation rate
- Customer reports caused by stale or conflicting profile facts

## Messaging

“MealScout gives your business a complete profile for discovery, current information, and supported customer actions. We call profile access a free trial, but it has no expiration, requires no card, never converts, and has no monthly bill.”

Do not measure trial-to-paid conversion or sell monthly profile upgrades. Improve profile completeness, truth, customer action, and merchant retention instead.
