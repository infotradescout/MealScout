# MealScout Founder Sales Pitch

## Short pitch

“MealScout gives your food business one complete profile where customers can find your current menu, location, availability, and supported actions. We call it a free trial, but it has no expiration, needs no card, never converts, and has no monthly bill. Let’s make your profile useful in one sitting.”

## Food-truck version

“MealScout connects your public profile, dated stops, live location, menu, and available ordering or booking actions. Customers should be able to trust where you are and what they can do next. The complete profile has no monthly bill.”

## Restaurant version

“MealScout gives the restaurant one current public profile for discovery, menu, hours, offers, and supported ordering. Complete profile tools are included with no monthly subscription.”

## Host version

“MealScout lets a host publish a venue profile and supported booking availability so food businesses can find and act on real opportunities. Any booking or payout charge is separate and shown in that transaction.”

## Objection: “I do not want another subscription.”

“You will not have one. Profile access is a non-expiring free trial with no card, no automatic conversion, and no monthly bill.”

## Close

“Give me the current menu or offerings, location or service area, availability, and best customer action. We’ll build the useful profile first.”

Never promise a particular transaction fee from memory. Use the current checkout or booking workflow as the source of truth.
