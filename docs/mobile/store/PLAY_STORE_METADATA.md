## Full Description

MealScout helps local diners and food operators stay in sync.

Discover nearby food deals, browse events, check what is live on the map, and place pickup orders from participating businesses.

For business operators, MealScout supports day-to-day execution with tools for schedule visibility, live location updates, event workflows, and operational tracking.

Core capabilities include:

- Local food and event discovery
- Menu browsing and pickup checkout
- Business profile and visibility tools
- Event participation coordination
- Operator-focused workflow and analytics surfaces

MealScout is designed to reduce manual work and make local food operations easier to manage.
